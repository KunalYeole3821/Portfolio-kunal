Sample · SEO case study

On-page audit: mid-size D2C site

A self-directed audit identifying quick-win on-page opportunities — title/meta rewrites, header restructuring, and internal-linking gaps — for a hypothetical D2C apparel site.

12pages audited
8quick-win fixes
3linking clusters mapped

Approach: Started with a content inventory to flag thin or duplicate pages, then mapped each page to a specific search intent (informational, commercial, transactional) before touching any copy.

What I changed: Rewrote meta titles/descriptions to match intent and include primary keywords naturally, restructured H1–H3 hierarchy for scannability, and proposed an internal-linking cluster connecting blog content to category pages.

Why it matters: Small on-page fixes compound — this is the kind of pass I run before recommending any larger content investment.

Sample project for demonstration — not affiliated with any real brand.

Sample · Writing

"How to Plan a Sorority Recruitment Event" — content brief + intro

A content brief and opening section written to match real search intent behind a long-tail, high-intent query — the kind of piece meant to earn clicks and time on page, not just rank.

850target word count
Guidecontent type

Search intent: The searcher is likely a chapter officer, weeks out from recruitment, overwhelmed and looking for a checklist more than inspiration.

Angle: A step-by-step planning guide organized by timeline (8 weeks out, 4 weeks out, week of), not a generic listicle — because that's how the searcher actually thinks about the problem.

Opening lines (sample): "Recruitment week always arrives faster than the planning calendar suggests. The chapters that pull it off calmly aren't the ones with the biggest budget — they're the ones who started with a checklist, not a Pinterest board."

Voice notes: Warm, direct, a little conversational — written for a college-age reader, not a corporate one.

Sample project for demonstration — written independently, no client affiliation.
